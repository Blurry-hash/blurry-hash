How we review a page

Take a closer look before drawing conclusions.

We try to read a page the way a thoughtful visitor would: understand what it offers, look at how the different parts fit together, and be clear about what the page does and does not tell us.

VANTA / SIGMA · A closer look GRID 01 / REV 02

There is no need to overcomplicate it. Read through the page, compare the sections, and explain what you noticed in your own words.

VANTA / SIGMA — A QUICK READING GUIDE

Start with the introduction. What does the business seem to offer?
Take a look at the services. Do they make the opening description clearer?
Read the approach section. Does it explain how the work is carried out?
Notice ideas or claims that show up in more than one place.
When you make an observation, mention what on the page gave you that impression.
If something is unclear or unavailable, say what you could not check.
Finish with a short, honest impression of the page as a whole.

A complete review is useful, but a partial review is fine too.
Just be open about what you were able to see and what remains uncertain.
